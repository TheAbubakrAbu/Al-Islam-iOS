Al-Islam is your complete companion for practicing Islam, whether you are a lifelong believer, a new convert, or simply exploring the faith. The Quran, the major Hadith collections, tafsir, prayer times, the Islamic calendar, and powerful learning tools come together in one beautifully simple app that is completely free, ad free, and private.

UNIQUE FEATURES
- Traveling Mode: Shorten prayers while traveling, automatically or manually, synced between iPhone and Apple Watch.
- Nagging Mode: Choose a start time before each prayer, then get reminders every 15 minutes until you pray.
- Arabic Beginner Mode: Spaces out each Arabic letter so they are easier to recognize while you learn to read.
- Verse Sharing: Share any verse as clean text or a beautiful image, or select several ayahs to share, copy, bookmark, or annotate together.
- Resume Anywhere: Jump back to your last read and last listened Surah and verse, with recent history saved.
- Powerful Search: Find any verse by Surah:Ayah (like 5:27), by page, by Juz, or by keyword across the whole Quran.

THE COMPLETE QURAN
- Read by Surah, Juz, or page, with dividers and a marker that always shows where you are.
- Arabic text, English transliteration, and translation side by side, with optional color coded tajweed and a tajweed reference.
- Tafsir for any ayah: Ibn Kathir, Maarif Ul Quran, and Tazkirul Quran in English, plus Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari, and as-Sa'di in Arabic, saved for offline reading.
- Compare the qiraat (readings) and English translations side by side.
- Listen to recitations from over 60 reciters, with full Surah and verse by verse playback, offline downloads, and offline ayah timings.

THE MAJOR HADITH COLLECTIONS
- The Nine Books: Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan an-Nasa'i, Sunan Abi Dawud, Jami` at-Tirmidhi, Sunan Ibn Majah, Muwatta Malik, Sunan ad-Darimi, and Musnad Ahmad.
- The forty-hadith collections (Nawawi, Qudsi, and Shah Waliullah, built into the app) plus Riyad as-Salihin, Mishkat al-Masabih, Bulugh al-Maram, Al-Adab Al-Mufrad, and Shama'il Muhammadiyah.
- Read in Arabic with English translation and narrator chains, with a Hadith of the Day, bookmarks, favorites, and reference lookups like "Bukhari 5".
- Search within any book or across every downloaded book, and save any collection for offline reading.

PRAYER TIMES AND QIBLA
- Accurate prayer times calculated privately on your device, with an Automatic method suggested for your region.
- Qibla compass, prayer notifications with pre alerts, and your choice of adhan sounds.
- Plan future prayer times, or look up any other city.

ISLAMIC CALENDAR AND EVENTS
- Hijri date in Arabic and English, with an adjustable offset and an option to advance the date at Maghrib.
- Islamic calendar with key events like Ramadan and Eid, plus notifications for important dates.

ESSENTIAL TOOLS AND LEARNING
- Authenticated adhkar and dua collections, with a Listen button that reads the Arabic aloud.
- Tasbih counter, the 99 Names of Allah, Masjid and Halal Food locators, a Hijri converter, and Islamic wallpapers.
- Learn the fundamentals of Islam: the Five Pillars, the Six Pillars of Faith, and core beliefs, explained simply for converts and beginners.

LEARN THE ARABIC ALPHABET
- Every Arabic letter, its forms, diacritics, and the Quranic stopping signs, with adjustable sizing and audio pronunciation.

MADE YOUR WAY
- Customize text sizes, Arabic fonts, accent colors, and prayer settings.
- Widgets and full Apple Watch support for prayer times on your home screen, lock screen, and wrist.

FREE, AD FREE, AND PRIVATE
Al-Islam is completely free. No ads, no fees, and no subscriptions. Your data never leaves your device.

Whether you want prayer times, the full Quran, the Hadith of the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him), or a deeper understanding of Islam, Al-Islam is your daily companion. Download it today and take the next step in your faith journey.
