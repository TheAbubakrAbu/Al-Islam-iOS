# Malay (ms): Bahasa Melayu (Malaysia), as Apple's Malay iOS and JAKIM write it

Malaysian Malay, NOT Indonesian: "boleh", "kerana", "pautan", "muat turun", "padam", "kongsi", "telefon",
"aplikasi" (Indonesian words like "unduh", "hapus", "bagikan", "tautan" are wrong here). Address the reader
as "anda" (lowercase, as Apple's Malay does), friendly and clear. Imperatives plain: "Ketik", "Pilih",
"Leret". Title Case for titles and buttons as Apple does.
