# German (de): as Apple's German iOS and German-speaking Muslims write it

For Muslims in Germany, Austria and Switzerland (many of Turkish, Arab, Bosnian, Albanian or Kurdish family,
many born there, many converts): standard German, neutral (no regionalisms; ß as in Germany). Address the
reader with "du" as Apple's German does, lowercase (du, dein, dich): "Tippe auf …", "Wähle …", "Aktiviere …",
warm and clear. Buttons and menu items short, in Apple's style, usually the infinitive or a noun ("Teilen",
"Löschen", "Notiz hinzufügen", "Lesezeichen"). Sentence case: only the first word and nouns are capitalised
("Arabische Schrift ausblenden"), never English Title Case.

**Typography:** German quotes „…“ (U+201E … U+201C); a label quoted in a sentence keeps its own words inside
them. A no-break space (U+00A0) between a number and % ("50 %": "%lld %%" with U+00A0) and inside units
("5 km"). ALL CAPS headers stay ALL CAPS (ß becomes SS). Compound nouns are written as one word or with a
hyphen, never with a space ("Gebetszeiten", "Koran-Einstellungen" or "Koraneinstellungen", "iCloud-Backup",
"Tajwid-Farben").
**Placeholders and case:** `%@` can be any word and cannot be declined: no article before it that must agree
("der %@"), no "vom %@" / "zum %@"; put a noun before it that you control ("Sure %@", "Gebet: %@", "von
Rezitator %@") or rephrase ("%@ wird geladen").
