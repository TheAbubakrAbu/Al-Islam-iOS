# Russian (ru): as Apple's Russian iOS and Russian-speaking Muslims write it

For Muslims in Russia (Tatarstan, Bashkortostan, the North Caucasus, the cities) and Central Asia. Address
the reader with lowercase "вы" (Коснитесь, Выберите, Включите), warm and clear; buttons short as Apple's
(Поделиться, Удалить, Скопировать). Sentence case for titles and buttons ("Добавить в избранное"). Write "е"
where Apple does (Еще, Темное), "ё" only where a word would be misread without it.

**Quotes:** «ёлочки» for quoting a label or a phrase; „лапки“ inside them if nested.
**Placeholders and case:** `%@` can be any word and cannot be declined. Never make it the object of a
preposition that needs a case ending you cannot add; rephrase with a nominative noun before it: "сура %@",
"чтец: %@", "в городе %@", "Время намаза: %@". Numbers: see the brief's plural rules (one / few / many / other).
