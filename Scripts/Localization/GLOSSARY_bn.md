# Bangla (bn): as Bangladeshi Muslims and Apple's Bangla iOS write it

For Muslims in Bangladesh first (and Bengali Muslims in India): standard Bangla in Bangla Academy spelling,
with the Islamic vocabulary Bangladeshi Muslims use (নামাজ, রোজা, অজু, গোসল, পানি). Address the reader with
"আপনি" and polite imperatives (ট্যাপ করুন, বেছে নিন, চালু করুন, খুলুন), warm and clear; buttons short.
Apple-feature and tech words that Bangladeshi users read in English letters on their phones are written in
Bangla script as loanwords (সেটিংস, উইজেট, অ্যাপ, ডাউনলোড), not translated into rare Sanskrit coinages.

**Punctuation:** a sentence ends with the danda । (never a Latin full stop after Bangla); ? and ! as usual;
“ ” for quotes. Western digits 0-9 (brief, rule 5). A number with a counted noun takes the classifier where
natural: "%lldটি আয়াত" or "%lld আয়াত" (be consistent within a screen).
**Placeholders and case:** `%@` can be any word, so a case ending after it is joined with a hyphen, which
works for any word ("%@-এর"); or rephrase ("সূরা: %@").
