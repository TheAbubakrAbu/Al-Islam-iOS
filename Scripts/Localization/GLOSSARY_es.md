# Spanish (es): as Apple's Spanish iOS and Spanish-speaking Muslims write it

For Muslims in Spain and Latin America, and Spanish-speaking Muslims in the US (many converts): one neutral
Spanish that reads naturally on both sides of the Atlantic. Address the reader with "tú" as Apple's Spanish
does ("Toca …", "Elige …", "Activa …"), warm and clear; never "vosotros" (a plural address, rare here, takes
"ustedes"), and avoid words only one region uses (no "ordenador", "móvil", "celular", "computadora": the app
names the device: iPhone, iPad). Buttons and menu items short, in Apple's style, usually the infinitive
("Compartir", "Eliminar", "Añadir nota"). Sentence case: only the first word and proper names are capitalised
("Ocultar puntos árabes"), never English Title Case.

**Arabic terms** keep the app's Latin transliteration (idgham, ikhfa, makhraj, khatm), the form the app's
legend and rule names show, rather than a Spanish respelling (not idgam, ijfa, jatm).

**Typography:** ¿…? and ¡…! always open; quotes “…” (a label quoted in a sentence keeps its own words inside
them). A no-break space (U+00A0) between a number and % ("50 %": "%lld %%" with U+00A0). Accents on
capitals (Ú, Á) in ALL CAPS headers too.
**Placeholders and gender:** `%@` can be any word, so avoid an article or contraction that must agree with it
("el %@", "del %@", "de %@" where %@ may start with "el"): put a noun before it you control ("la sura %@",
"Sura: %@", "Recitador: %@") or rephrase.
