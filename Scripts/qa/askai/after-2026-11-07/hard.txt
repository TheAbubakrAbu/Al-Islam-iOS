A: The shortening (Qasr) of prayers while traveling is permitted, but its permissibility and how it is applied depend on the prayer and the journey's circumstances.

1. Shortening is allowed for Dhuhr, Asr, and Isha: two rak'ah each [2].
2. Fajr remains two rak'ah as usual [2].
3. Maghrib is three rak'ah as usual [2].
4. The rawatib (additional prayers) are dropped on a journey, except the two before Fajr and Witr, which the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) never left [2].
5. Voluntary prayers are still permitted [2].
6. The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) once commanded that the two before Fajr and Witr be completed behind a resident imam, and Ibn Abbas said this is the Sunnah of Abu al-Qasim [2].
7. A scholar of the hadith, `Abdullah bin `Umar, narrated that the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said, "Anyone of you attending the Friday (prayers) should take a bath" [6].
8. The scholar `Umar ibn `Abd al-Rahman (from the same hadith) is recorded as saying, "By God, if Thou hadst not guided us aright, We would have neither practised charity nor offered prayers" [4].
9. A scholar named Salim bin 'Abdullah was asked about combining prayers while traveling and replied, "No, except at Jam" [3] [8].
10. Another scholar, Safiyyah bint Abi 'Ubaid, wrote to Salim bin 'Abdullah: "This is the last of my days in this world, and the first day of the Hereafter" [3] [8].

There is scholarly disagreement on deliberately leaving prayers undone. Ibn Baz and Ibn Uthaymeen held that abandoning them altogether is among the gravest of sins, and al-Albani said it is not unless the obligation is denied [5]. The Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) described negligence of prayer as the boundary of faith [5].

For guidance tailored to your situation, consult a qualified scholar.

A: The nisab is the minimum amount of wealth that a person must hold before they are obligated to give zakat. According to the app, this includes cash and savings, gold and silver, money owed that is expected to be repaid, business goods, and investments held for profit, but it does not count personal items like a home or car [3].

The Quran clarifies that zakat is not about fulfilling a fixed amount but about giving wealth in a way that pleases Allah [1]. The Prophet is reported to have stated that zakat is a means of raising one's status and that even giving as little as half a date can save a person from Hell [2] [5]. The app also notes that the Prophet wrote a decree that certain animals are not eligible for zakat unless the collector chooses otherwise [5].

The Prophet is said to have gone to a garden to drink water, and this reflects the importance of giving freely [7].
